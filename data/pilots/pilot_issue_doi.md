# Citation Quality Analysis in Technical Reports

## Abstract
We examine bibliographic consistency in automated review pipelines. This study focuses on reference formatting and identifier resolution.

## Introduction
Accurate citations are foundational to scientific rigor. When papers include duplicated or malformed references, traceability breaks down.

## Methodology
We analyzed citation corpora to detect duplicated bibliographic entries and broken identifiers across published conference proceedings.

## Results
Duplicate citations appeared in 4% of analyzed drafts, while malformed identifiers represented 2% of total references.

## Discussion
Automated linters can easily flag duplicate citations before final publication, saving substantial editorial effort.
