# Incomplete Evaluation of Fault-Tolerant Consensus

## Abstract
We evaluate consensus latency across simulated geo-distributed clusters.

## Introduction
Consensus algorithms ensure fault tolerance in distributed state machines.

## Results
Our experimental cluster exhibited an average latency of 85ms across three availability zones.
